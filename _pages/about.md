Welcome! I am a Senior Lecturer (Associate Professor) in Comparative Politics at the [University of Bath](https://www.bath.ac.uk/departments/department-of-politics-languages-international-studies/). My research focuses on gender, political representation, and political parties, particularly in Western Europe.

In 2026, I was awarded a [UKRI Future Leaders Fellowship](https://www.bath.ac.uk/announcements/two-university-of-bath-researchers-awarded-ukri-future-leader-fellowships/) for *Invisible Burdens, Visible Gaps: The Mental Load and Gendered Citizenship*. The project explores how the often-invisible work of planning and managing everyday life shapes gender inequalities in political participation and public life. Other projects I am excited to be working on explore women and gender in populist radical right parties, the rise of femonationalism, and gender affinity in politics.

My book, [Making Gender Salient: From Gender Quota Laws to Policy](/book/), was published by Cambridge University Press in 2022. My research has appeared in journals including *American Journal of Political Science*, *American Political Science Review*, *British Journal of Political Science*, *Comparative Political Studies*, *The Journal of Politics*, and *Journal of Marriage and Family*.

I am an Editor at *Legislative Studies Quarterly* and Lead Co-Editor of [Cambridge Studies in Gender & Politics](https://www.cambridge.org/core/series/cambridge-studies-in-gender-and-politics/3F1CE6BE0E125F59DA8D2DBF736B400B), with Rachel E. Brulé. Please get in touch if you are interested in submitting a book manuscript.

I have been honored to receive several awards for my research, including the [2024 CES Carolina de Miguel Moyer Young Scholar Award](https://councilforeuropeanstudies.org/2024-awardee/), the 2023 APSA Women, Gender, and Politics Research Section Best Paper Award for [The Political Consequences of the Mental Load](https://doi.org/10.1093/esr/jcaf019), and the [2018 APSA Franklin L. Burdette / Pi Sigma Alpha Award](https://politicalsciencenow.com/ana-catalano-weeks-2018-franklin-l-burdette-pi-sigma-alpha-award-recipient/) for my research on why gender quota laws are adopted by men.

Before joining Bath, I was a College Fellow in the Department of Government at Harvard University and a Research Fellow in the Women and Public Policy Program at the Harvard Kennedy School. I have a PhD in Political Science from Harvard University, an MSc in Comparative Politics (Europe) from the LSE, and an AB in Government from Dartmouth College.

<p>My publications and research agenda reflect my interests in gender, representation, political parties, and the politics of policymaking. Projects I am excited to be working on explore women and gender in populist radical right parties, femonationalism, gender quotas, and the political causes and consequences of the mental load (cognitive household labor). Most articles below are open access or have linked PDFs. If you cannot access anything, please <a href="mailto:a.c.weeks@bath.ac.uk">email me</a>.</p>
